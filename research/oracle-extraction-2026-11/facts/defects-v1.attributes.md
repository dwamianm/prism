# defects-v1: `has` attributes

One meaning per attribute, used across the whole conversation.

- `trip_timing`: when the trip happens, as the turn words it ("late March next year").
- `trip_season`: a season the trip falls in, named by the assistant ("cherry blossom season").

No other `has` attribute is used. Everything else is a relation of the vocabulary: `partner_of`, `involved_in`,
`located_in`, `decided`, `prefers`, `allergic_to`, `uses`, `manager_of`, `part_of`.
